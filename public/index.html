Why Sinta exists

Sinta was built for people in the Philippines dealing with a rising wave of scams that lean on leaked personal data — a text that knows your name, a call that knows your address. Knowing whether your email has already been exposed is the first step to protecting yourself.

This is a small, independent tool — not a bank, telco, or government service.

How your privacy is handled: By checking, your email is sent directly from your browser to XposedOrNot, a free public breach-lookup service, over an encrypted connection. This tool keeps no copy of what you search or the results.
